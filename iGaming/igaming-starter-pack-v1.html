04 / 20

Money flow

These numbers are not interchangeable.

Deposit — money added to wallet.

Turnover — total amount wagered.

Player win — settled returns to player.

GGR — gaming result before defined deductions.

Withdrawal — money removed from wallet.

NGR — net revenue after defined deductions.

Deposit ≠ Turnover ≠ GGR

A player can deposit 100 and wager the same balance repeatedly until turnover reaches 500. Each number answers a different question.

Starter glossary

Know the language, but always confirm how your company defines each field.
FTDFirst-time depositor.
GGRGross gaming revenue under the reporting definition used.
NGRNet gaming revenue after defined deductions; definition varies.
TurnoverTotal amount wagered.
RTPTheoretical long-run return to player.
CPAFixed qualifying acquisition payment.
Revenue ShareCommission based on an agreed share of defined revenue.
CRMCustomer relationship management / lifecycle communication.
KYCKnow Your Customer checks.
PSPPayment service provider.
LTVEstimated lifetime value.
Active PlayerPlayer meeting the operator's defined activity rule.
Bonus RatioBonus value compared with a defined base; calculation varies.
Net DepositUsually deposits minus withdrawals for a scope; confirm definition.
Wagering RequirementPlaythrough condition attached to some promotional value.

The newcomer rule

When you see a metric, ask: What does it measure? How is it calculated? What period does it cover? What decision is it supposed to support?
