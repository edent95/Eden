🍀 What is a Free PT Campaign? 🍀 什么是 Free PT 活动？
“Free PT” stands for Free Play Trial. In this campaign, providers allow operators to run their games for 1 month with no royalty fees. It’s like a free trial — operators can test performance before deciding whether to continue the game.

🎯 Think of it like a free food sample: if players like it, the operator may keep it. “Free PT” 指的是 免费试玩活动。在此活动中，游戏商允许运营商在 第一个月免分成 上架其游戏。就像是一个免费试用期，运营商可以先观察游戏表现，再决定是否继续合作。

🎯 想象成超市里的试吃样品，如果玩家喜欢，运营商就会决定继续上架。
